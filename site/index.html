Review on every board

Ada argues against its own design before you see it, and drops any finding the circuit does not back.

HIGH Serial header TX and RX wired straight through

TXD0 lands on the header pin named TXD. On an FTDI adapter that pin is an output too, so two drivers fight and both receivers float.

fix: cross the pair, TXD0 to header RXD

HIGH No pull-ups on SDA and SCL

The Qwiic port hangs off IO21 and IO22 with nothing pulling either line to 3.3 V. I2C is open-drain, so the bus never idles high.

fix: 4.7 kΩ from SDA and SCL to +3V3

HIGH The header's 3.3 V pin backfeeds the regulator

The header shares the regulator's output net with no diode, so a 3.3 V cable and USB-C plugged in together fight each other.

fix: a Schottky or a solder jumper on that pin
